# OneNode
Your perfect ecosystem maker across all your devices! Seamlessly share all kinds of files across your devices in the local network! No cloud, no database, ensuring complete privacy and faster transfer speed!

## OneNode is still in development

I know that you're quiet excited, but wait for a few more days! We will have something that certainly rewards your curiosity! We are building something great and it requires a litle bit more of time :)


`How does OneNode stand out?`
- OneNode makes sure that none of your files nor transfer data is leaked out to anyone. We don't use any file storage mechanisms or send files over the cloud and internet. Everything under control, with total transparency :)

`Now, what's unique with OneNode?`
- OneNode has a very small footprint over the disk. We are constantly trying to reduce the footprint, while providing as much comfort to each of it's user. It was specifically designed to help constrained and low end devices (we got you!)

`Who can use OneNode?`
- OneNode can be used by anyone, quite literally! With a minimal interface, it is very much convenient for anyone with no clue on what's going around. We also have a CLI for developers who want to go terminal and rock around! Are you someone who customizes a lot? We didn't leave you out bud! We don't lie when we say our product can be used by anyone!

`When can I use OneNode?`
- Whenever you got some files and photos to transfer from your phone to your laptop, or send some chunky childhood videos to your family, OneNode is always there to help you out! With cloud, you have to upload, wait, then download and wait even more. OneNode never makes you wait. We stop the world to wait for you (hehe!)
